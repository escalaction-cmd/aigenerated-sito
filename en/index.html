Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is Vedeasy di Castronovo Giuseppe, Via Aosta 4, 20155 Milan, Italy, Tax code: CSTGPP76D15G273A, VAT no.: 03124310842. For any question write to info@vedeasy.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect personal data only when you fill in the "Your project" form: first name, last name, email address, phone number, role, company and the text you write about your project.

While you write, this data stays on this page, on your device: we do not send it to anyone. It leaves only when you press "Book a 30-minute call". There is no registration, there is no newsletter, and we do not collect browsing data for statistics or advertising. Like every website, the service that hosts this page automatically records some technical data about visits, such as IP address, date and time, to keep the site secure and working.

3. Why we use it and on what basis

  • To reply to your request and schedule the call. The legal basis is the request you make to us, that is, pre-contractual measures (Article 6, paragraph 1, letter b of Regulation (EU) 2016/679).
  • For the security and operation of the site, using the technical data of the server. The legal basis is our legitimate interest (Article 6, paragraph 1, letter f).

We do not use your data to send you advertising and we do not use it to profile you. We do not sell it.

4. Booking the call

Calls are booked on Cal.com, an external service. When you press the button, its booking page opens in a new tab, where the name, email and notes (project, phone, role and company) arrive already filled in. From that moment the booking data is also processed by that service, to schedule the appointment and send you the confirmation, according to its own privacy notice.

5. Who can see the data

We see it. Some providers help us: whoever hosts this page and our email, and the booking service. We do not share the data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

For the time needed to reply to and handle your request. If a working relationship does not start, we delete it within 12 months. If one does start, we keep it for its whole duration and for the periods the law requires for tax and accounting obligations. The technical data of the server is kept for a short period, set by the hosting provider.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, object to the processing or receive it in a readable format (from Article 15 to Article 22 of the Regulation). Write to info@vedeasy.com. If you think your rights are not respected you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

8. Cookies

This page does not use profiling cookies, does not use cookies for statistics and does not load third party tools to measure visits or run advertising. The fonts, images and videos are hosted on the page itself, without calling external services. For this reason there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to accept or refuse.

When you move to the booking page, that service may use its own cookies: the details are in its privacy notice.

9. Content generated with artificial intelligence

The sample images, videos and flyers on this page are generated with artificial intelligence and are labelled as such.

10. Changes

If something changes, for example if we add a statistics tool, we update this page and the date at the top.
