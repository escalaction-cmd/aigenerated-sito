    Legal checks

    Price rules, checked row by row

    Before laying out, Folifly checks every offer against Italian price advertising rules and flags what is missing.

    30 days

    Previous price

    Art. 17-bis, Italian Consumer Code

    The discount is calculated on the lowest price of the previous 30 days. If the file does not confirm it, the offer is held. Fresh produce follows its own rule.

    10 days · 50 items

    Below cost sales

    Presidential Decree 218/2001

    At most 10 days and 50 items, with available quantities and the per customer limit printed on the page.

    €/kg · €/l

    Unit price

    Italian Consumer Code

    The price per kilo, litre or piece next to every price, calculated from the pack size.

    The checks help your team and do not replace legal advice. Notice to the municipality and the limit of three below cost sales a year are verified outside the platform.

    The platform

    A space for head office, one for every store

    The platform is invitation only. Head office sets up banners, catalogue, stores and locks; each store sees its own flyer and changes only what is left open.

    • Invitation onlyNo public sign up: head office invites people and chooses their role.
    • Separate rolesHead office and stores see different screens. Locks are enforced on the server too.
    • Separate dataEach chain's catalogue, images and flyers stay its own.
    • Work savedEvery change saves itself; flyers are published to stores and archived.
